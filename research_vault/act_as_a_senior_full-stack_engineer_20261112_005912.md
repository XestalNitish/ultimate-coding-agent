API Reference
Everything you need to integrate Resume Parser into your application. Sign up with your email, create an API key in the dashboard, and send your first PDF.
Overview
One endpoint: send a PDF, get structured JSON back in the same response.
Base URL: https://api.resumeparser.cc
Authentication: send your key as Authorization: Bearer rp_live_.... Keys are created in the dashboard after you sign up with your email. Keep the key on your server, never in browser code.
Limits: PDF only, up to 5 MB. 30 requests per minute per key, and at most 2 parses running at once per key. Requests rejected before parsing (for example 402, 413 or 429) do not count toward the rate limit. Parsing is synchronous and can take up to 60 seconds, so set your HTTP client timeout to at least 60 seconds.
No OCR: scanned PDFs without a text layer return 422.
Billing: 50 free parses per month, then prepaid credits bought by card in the dashboard (1 credit = 1 successful parse): EUR 0.15 per parse for 100 to 1,999 credits, EUR 0.12 for 2,000 to 9,999, EUR 0.10 for 10,000 or more, excluding VAT. Credits never expire and failed parses are not charged.
